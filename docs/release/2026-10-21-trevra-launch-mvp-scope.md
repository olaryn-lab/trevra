# Trevra — launch-critical scope, not Dripify parity

**Decision date:** 2026-10-21. **Owner direction:** ship a dependable product first; defer feature-complete Dripify cloning and large adjacent projects. This document supersedes the parity tracker **for launch priority only**, not as evidence that unfinished parity tasks are done.

## Target release

**Small, founder-assisted launch** of hosted Trevra for a limited set of consenting early customers. One real owner workspace, a working linked LinkedIn companion/account, a verified sender mailbox, CSV lead intake, one sequence/campaign, explicit approval, paced outbound actions, reply/opt-out handling, campaign stop/pause, basic activity visibility and manual support. A paid early customer may be handled through owner-assisted invoicing **only if the agreed commercial/legal process permits it**. Do not advertise public self-service billing or free trials when not enforced.

No claim of full Dripify parity, unlimited automation, autonomous LinkedIn scraping, or provider integration that has not passed live acceptance. LinkedIn platform terms and permitted use must be reviewed before activation; companion/browser location does not by itself establish permission.

## Specifically deferred until after initial launch

- TRV-039 AI composing; TRV-051 CRM; TRV-052 outbound webhooks (foundation already exists but leave disabled unless independently approved); TRV-053 public API; TRV-045 full teams/roles; TRV-047 agencies/white-label.
- TRV-021 automatic LinkedIn search, TRV-022 Sales Navigator scraping, TRV-023 engagers/evergreen, advanced analytics/A-B testing, every campaign/template variant, cross-channel expansion, revenue attribution.
- TRV-057 automated self-service subscription/trial lifecycle **only for the founder-assisted launch**; manual commercial operations must be honest and auditable. For a self-serve paid launch, entitlements, renewals, failed payments and access restrictions become required before public activation.
- Remote/cloud LinkedIn browser mode, if the paired companion route is the supported first-launch execution home.
- Strict full-source Dripify-parity certification, any blanket open-source licensing claim, and SBOM marketing. The invalid npm dependency graph is still technical debt to repair for reproducible builds; never publish a stale SBOM or claim verified dependency provenance.

## Engineering execution order from now on

1. **Launch blockers only:** close L1–L6 first, beginning with static/isolated qualification and then controlled staging and owner-authorized live provider acceptance; never assume provider access or claim a live success from unit tests.
2. Close L7–L8 and perform one complete customer journey with a clean account and a real permitted sender/channel. Fix bugs found in that journey before adding features.
3. Run accepted migration, data recovery, rollback and monitoring checks in staging; record evidence and sign-off. Deployment itself requires owner authorization and suitable credentials.
4. Launch a small cohort, measure failures and support load, and only then reopen noncritical Dripify parity backlog.

**Tooling distinction:** `npm run release:certify` intentionally remains a **full-parity** release gate and may report 56+ blockers. It must not be bypassed or relabeled as having passed. The launch decision uses L1–L8 backed by actual evidence, not a count of unrelated unbuilt features. Do not mark any L gate VERIFIED based only on this document.
