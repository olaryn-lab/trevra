# Trevra LinkedIn: production readiness tracker

Owner: Trevra. Started: 2026-10-27. Working branch: `feat/linkedin-production-readiness-20261027`. Source of truth for the current work; update this document after each verified milestone. Do not treat a passing mock/browser transport test as proof a real message was sent.

## Goal

An approved LinkedIn campaign reliably executes while a paired Companion is available, stops following up when a prospect replies, survives disconnections without duplicates, and shows a truthful plain-English reason when nothing happens. Do not silently replay legacy campaigns or contact arbitrary prospects in tests.

## Non-negotiable safety

- Production has two intentionally paused historical campaigns, 37 held actions, and one claimed unknown-outcome action from older outreach. Preserve those states until the owner explicitly approves a reconciliation.
- No real outbound invitation/message without an approved recipient and action content.
- No silent relaxation of account limits, working hours, opt-outs, approvals or seat assignment.
- Keep Companion authenticated and session/profile confined to the user's computer. Browser automation is subject to LinkedIn platform restrictions; infrastructure reliability does not remove that commercial risk.

## Current findings

1. Separate planning/outbound/maintenance timers and a FIFO browser lane are deployed, along with scheduler health reporting and first-maintenance-before-outbound startup ordering (main `49f1475`).
2. The existing inbox cadence marks visits even on failures, and scheduled inbox maintenance does not guarantee freshness before later outbound follow-ups.
3. The send-time database checks detect replies already ingested into the action ledger, but cannot know about replies which have not yet been read from LinkedIn.
4. Production send reliability remains unproven without a real owner-approved test action.
