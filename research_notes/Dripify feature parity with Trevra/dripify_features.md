## 1. Campaign / sequence builder: actions, conditional logic, builder UX, templates, A/B testing

### Takeaway

Dripify has a visual, branching sequence builder (marketed as drag-and-drop) for mixed LinkedIn and email sequences. It offers about 12 action types, 4 explicit conditions, built-in reply or acceptance branches on certain actions, and delays measured in hours or days. Active campaigns can be edited live. It has 18+ pre-built templates filtered by lead source, plus saved and team templates. A/B testing (Beta, Advanced only) supports up to 3 variants on Message, Send Invite, Email, and Sales Navigator InMail. There are no per-campaign time windows; scheduling is an account-level "working hours" setting.

### Cited Findings

**Actions available in a sequence (confirmed)**

- Official action list (updated 2026-08-05): send connection requests, message leads, endorse skills, view profiles, follow, unfollow, like a post, and send emails. Delays and "triggers" can be placed between actions. — [HELP Actions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028711-what-actions-can-be-added-to-your-sequence)
- The marketing FAQ gives the fuller list: connection request, LinkedIn message, Sales Navigator InMail, email, Find Email, View profile, Follow/Unfollow, Like a post, Endorse skills, and Withdraw pending invitation. It describes this as "15+ actions & conditions". — [MKT Drip campaigns](https://dripify.com/features/drip-campaigns/)
- **Connection request ("Send invite") with or without a note.** The personalized-note field is **disabled on free LinkedIn accounts** because LinkedIn caps free accounts at about 5–10 personalized invites per month. Premium accounts have no such cap. — [HELP Free-account note limits](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/8490987-limited-personalized-connection-request-notes-for-free-linkedin-accounts)
- Invite note length is limited to 300 characters on Premium, and LinkedIn reduced it to 200 characters on free accounts. A note over the limit fails the action. — [HELP Failed actions (updated 2026-10-12)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10095836-addressing-failed-actions-on-dripify); [HELP Custom fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16051949-how-to-create-and-use-custom-lead-fields)
- **Message** works with 1st-degree connections only, up to 5,000 characters per message (LinkedIn itself allows 8,000). — [HELP Char limit](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10475460-what-s-the-maximum-character-limit-in-dripify-messages); [MKT Personalization FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/hyper-personalized-outreach/)
- **Sales Navigator InMail.** This replaced "Basic LinkedIn InMail" in July 2026, and Basic InMail is no longer supported. The action requires a connected Sales Navigator contract. InMails to Open Profiles use no credits; non-Open Profiles use the 50/month credits (rollover up to 150). Each InMail action automatically creates "Responded to InMail" and "Still not responded to InMail" branches, and multiple InMail actions can be used per sequence. Limits are 200 characters for the subject and 1,885 for the body. Locked Sales Navigator profiles must be unlocked manually (LinkedIn allows 25 unlocks per month). — [HELP InMail (updated 2026-10-12)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6067993-how-to-send-inmails-with-dripify); [PU July 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15902276-product-update-july-2026); [HELP Failed actions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10095836-addressing-failed-actions-on-dripify)
- **Endorse skills** works with 1st-degree connections only. If there are no skills to endorse, the action is processed as a success rather than a failure. — [HELP Failed actions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10095836-addressing-failed-actions-on-dripify)
- **Like a post.** If there are no posts to like, the action is processed as a success. — same source.
- **Follow** fails on non-followable or already-followed profiles. **Unfollow** (added August 2026) has no settings, can only be placed where the lead is followed or follow status is unknown, and is silently skipped if it fails. — [HELP Unfollow](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16219413-how-to-automatically-unfollow-leads); [PU Aug 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16384791-product-update-august-2026)
- **Withdraw invite.** Available as a sequence action (added October 2022), or as an end-of-sequence option "Withdraw requests if not accepted yet" after a delay such as 30 days. Re-inviting the same lead requires a gap of more than 21 days after withdrawal, and the builder enforces the 21-day gap when saving. — [HELP Auto-withdraw](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5034118-how-to-automatically-withdraw-your-connection-requests); [HELP Re-invite](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6657214-how-to-send-an-invite-a-second-time-to-the-same-lead); [PU Oct 2022](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6613543-product-update-october-2022); [MKT Drip campaigns](https://dripify.com/features/drip-campaigns/)
- **Find Email** is a sequence action that splits leads into found and not found. **Send Email** automatically inserts an "If email available" condition. — [HELP Find email](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/7924386-how-to-search-for-a-lead-s-verified-email); [HELP Send email](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/9134046-how-to-send-automated-emails-via-dripify)
- **Messaging LinkedIn Group members is deliberately not supported**, because of LinkedIn's limit of about 10 messages per month. — [HELP Groups](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15805363-how-to-add-leads-from-linkedin-groups)
- **Not supported:** automated birthday, work-anniversary, or new-job messages (confirmed as unavailable). — [HELP Birthday](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10401175-can-dripify-automate-birthday-wishes-work-anniversaries-or-new-job-congratulations)
- **Deprecated:** the "Invite by email" action was removed for new campaigns in January 2025, together with its stats, filters, and webhook triggers. Existing campaigns keep running. — [PU Jan 2025](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10353422-product-update-january-2025)

**Conditional logic**

- There are four explicit conditions: **If connected**, **If viewed message**, **If email is available**, and **If open profile** (introduced December 2022). Each condition is a one-time check at a specific moment, so a lead does not switch branches if its status changes later. — [HELP Conditions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6788403-how-to-use-conditions); [PU Dec 2022](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6803673-product-update-december-2022)
- Some actions create their own branches: Send invite (accepted or not accepted), InMail (responded or not), and Find Email (found or not). Leads can wait in an "Awaiting" state until an invite is accepted, an InMail is replied to, or a message is viewed. — [HELP Lead progress](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6481719-track-the-progress-of-your-leads-in-a-campaign); [HELP InMail](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6067993-how-to-send-inmails-with-dripify)
- **Delay** steps can be set in hours or days. A delay cannot be the first step. — [HELP Sequence types](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10163922-what-is-a-sequence-and-how-to-choose-the-right-one); [HELP Delay first step](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10401118-can-i-add-a-delay-before-the-1st-step-in-the-sequence)
- **Auto-stop on reply.** A reply to a message, invite note, or InMail pauses the lead ("Replied/Paused"). Email replies (Gmail/Outlook, since July 2026) and Sales Navigator InMail replies pause the lead **across all campaigns**. Accepting an invite without replying does **not** pause the lead. Sending a manual message from the Dripify inbox also pauses the lead. — [HELP Sequence types](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10163922-what-is-a-sequence-and-how-to-choose-the-right-one); [HELP Resume](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5914465-how-to-resume-a-sequence-for-a-lead-that-replied-to-your-message); [HELP Email replies](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15502759-how-dripify-handles-email-replies)
- Once a lead completes a sequence, nothing more happens. For example, if the lead accepts the invite after finishing, they do not move into the "accepted" branch. — [HELP Sequence types](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10163922-what-is-a-sequence-and-how-to-choose-the-right-one)
- Time windows are set as account-level **working hours** (Settings). No per-step send-time windows are documented. — [PU Feb 2025](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10631840-product-update-february-2025); [HELP Avoid blocks](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12821690-how-to-avoid-being-blocked-or-flagged-by-linkedin)
- Every branch must be closed before launch. Best-practice guidance is no more than 3 of the same action per sequence, to avoid bottlenecks. — [HELP Create sequence](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028684-step-3-how-to-create-a-sequence); [HELP Sequence types](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10163922-what-is-a-sequence-and-how-to-choose-the-right-one)

**Builder UX, live editing, queues**

- Marketing describes a "Drag-and-drop sequence builder" with "15+ actions & conditions" (the homepage says "10+"). — [MKT llms.txt](https://dripify.com/llms.txt); [MKT Drip campaigns](https://dripify.com/features/drip-campaigns/)
- **Live editing** (November 2025, expanded July 2026). In active or paused campaigns you can add View profile, Like, Follow, Message, Endorse, or Unfollow at the start or between steps. You can remove "End of sequence" to extend a branch, which pulls completed leads back in. You can delete simple non-branching steps, but conditions and complex actions cannot be deleted, and at least one action must remain. Message, InMail, and Email text and delays are editable. Leads on a deleted step move forward. — [HELP Edit campaign (updated 2026-07-02)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6089903-how-to-edit-your-existing-campaign-or-sequence-in-dripify); [PU Nov 2025](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12808987-product-update-november-2025); [PU July 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15902276-product-update-july-2026)
- A **leads queue per step** shows waiting, failed, and completed leads. From the queue you can remove leads, edit lead data, export to CSV, and see failure reasons (14 documented reasons, including duplicate message at more than 90% similarity, not a 1st-degree connection, and missing profile variable). — [HELP Leads queue](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6068095-understanding-the-leads-queue-in-dripify-sequences); [HELP Failed actions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10095836-addressing-failed-actions-on-dripify)
- **Multiple campaigns can run at once** on Pro and Advanced (Basic is limited to one). Daily limits are shared and split equally across active campaigns. — [HELP Multiple campaigns](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5034187-how-to-run-multiple-campaigns-simultaneously); [MKT Drip campaigns](https://dripify.com/features/drip-campaigns/)
- Unlimited campaigns and unlimited actions per sequence on Pro and Advanced. — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)

**Templates**

- **Pre-built templates** (redesigned February 2026) are grouped by use case: Generate leads (7, including "Page followers win flow" and "Event attendee outreach"), Recruit talents (4), Build connections (3), and Engage with the network (4, including "Company follow request" and "Podcast/Webinar invite"). The template list is filtered automatically by lead source, and templates use [BRACKET] placeholders. — [HELP Templates (updated 2026-02-17)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12478338-how-to-use-templates-in-dripify); [PU Feb 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/13750387-product-update-february-2026)
- Marketing counts "18+ pre-built drip campaign templates". — [MKT Drip campaigns](https://dripify.com/features/drip-campaigns/)
- **Saved sequence templates** live in Settings → Sequence Templates or come from "Save as template". **Team Templates** can be created by Managers or Owners only. Whole campaigns, including leads, cannot be copied between teammates. You cannot save a sequence as a template if any action has A/B testing on, and team templates cannot contain custom-field variables (both planned). — [HELP Templating](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028710-templating-campaigns); [HELP A/B](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16071414-a-b-testing-beta); [HELP Custom fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16051949-how-to-create-and-use-custom-lead-fields)

**A/B testing (Beta, Advanced plan only)**

- Up to **3 variants** per action, auto-named A/B/C. Leads are split randomly across enabled variants. Each variant has its own sent, reply, and failed stats. You can toggle or delete variants without losing their history. As of 2026-10-12 it supports **Message, Send Invite, Email, and Sales Navigator InMail**. Send Invite A/B testing needs a paid LinkedIn plan. — [HELP A/B (updated 2026-10-12)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16071414-a-b-testing-beta)
- Rollout timeline: Messages (August 2026), then Email and Send Invite (September 2026), then InMail (documented in the InMail article by October 2026). — [PU Aug 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16384791-product-update-august-2026); [PU Sep 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16938124-product-update-september-2026); [HELP InMail](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6067993-how-to-send-inmails-with-dripify)

### Inferences

- Conditions are limited to LinkedIn and email state checks. No conditions are documented on lead attributes such as title or industry, on tags, on custom fields, or on email opens or clicks. Branching is driven by events, not data.
- "Tag lead" is not available as a sequence action. The Tags article says it is coming in future updates.

### Gaps

- Delay granularity (minimum delay, or whether minutes are possible) is not documented beyond "hours or days".
- I found no confirmation of an "Invite to follow company page" action, even though a "Company follow request" template exists. That template may just use profile-level actions.
- How "drag-and-drop" the builder really is (free canvas versus a click-to-add tree) cannot be confirmed without app access.

## 2. Lead sourcing: imports, Sales Navigator, CSV, engagers, events, groups, list management, dedupe, blacklist

### Takeaway

Dripify markets "9 lead sources". Confirmed sources are LinkedIn search, Sales Navigator (search, saved search, and lead list), Recruiter, My Network, LinkedIn Events, LinkedIn Groups (since July 2026), post likers and commenters (since May 2026), CSV, and pasted profile URLs. Leads can also be added via the Open API and MCP (since September 2026). Evergreen auto-refresh (Advanced only) covers post engagement (every 12 hours) and Sales Navigator (weekly). Dedupe and blacklist controls exist at the list, campaign, and team level.

### Cited Findings

- **LinkedIn search URL import** works for Basic/free, Sales Navigator, and Recruiter. It also covers **My Network** and **LinkedIn Events**. The limit is 1,000 leads per search on free or Recruiter accounts and 2,475 on Sales Navigator. — [HELP Add leads (updated 2026-07-21)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028652-step-1-how-to-add-leads-to-a-campaign)
- **CSV import or pasted profile URLs.** The CSV must contain LinkedIn profile URLs. CSV upload includes column-to-field mapping, a "Skip unmapped fields" option, and three modes for existing leads: Update all, Update missing fields only (the default), or Do not update. Each CSV cell is limited to 1,000 characters. — [HELP Add leads](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028652-step-1-how-to-add-leads-to-a-campaign); [HELP Custom fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16051949-how-to-create-and-use-custom-lead-fields)
- **Sales Navigator.** All URL types are supported: Search, Saved Search, and Lead List. Sales Navigator Advanced accounts with corporate SSO connect through the Chrome extension (since June 2026). A free LinkedIn account cannot import from Sales Navigator or Recruiter URLs. — [HELP Evergreen](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/14181907-how-evergreen-lead-upload-works-in-dripify); [HELP SN Advanced](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/11408275-how-to-use-a-sales-navigator-advanced-account-with-dripify); [HELP Contracts](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10164810-understanding-linkedin-contracts-what-they-are-and-the-different-types)
- **Documented workaround for sharing one Sales Navigator seat.** Import with the Sales Navigator account, export to CSV, then import the CSV into teammates' accounts that use free LinkedIn. — [HELP SN free-account workaround](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5612509-how-to-add-leads-from-sales-navigator-if-you-have-a-free-linkedin-account)
- **Post engagers (May 2026).** Any public post works: your own, sponsored or ads, competitor company posts, reposts, and embedded posts. Choose likes (all reactions combined, up to 3,000) or direct comments (up to 500, replies excluded) per list. Company pages are skipped automatically. Recruiter accounts and group posts are not supported. — [HELP Posts (updated 2026-05-27)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12658858-how-to-add-leads-from-linkedin-posts); [PU May 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15272237-product-update-may-2026)
- **Group members (July 2026).** You must be a member of the group, then pick it from a dropdown. Messaging group members directly is not supported. — [HELP Groups](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15805363-how-to-add-leads-from-linkedin-groups); [PU July 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15902276-product-update-july-2026)
- **Your followers and competitors' followers** are sourced through documented search-URL recipes (the "Followers of" filter, or Sales Navigator "Following your company"). They are not dedicated importers. — [HELP Followers](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/11813412-how-to-export-your-linkedin-followers-into-dripify); [HELP Competitor followers](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/13428773-how-to-find-competitors-page-followers-using-sales-navigator)
- **Evergreen Lead Upload (Advanced only).** Post engagement is re-checked every 12 hours, during working hours. Sales Navigator search, saved search, or list is re-checked weekly (since September 2026, new campaigns only). Each list holds at most 10,000 leads. Evergreen pauses when the campaign pauses and is disabled if you downgrade. — [HELP Evergreen (updated 2026-08-19)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/14181907-how-evergreen-lead-upload-works-in-dripify); [PU Sep 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16938124-product-update-september-2026)
- **Lead list filters**, applied at import and editable later: same leads in other campaigns, same leads in teammates' campaigns, no open profile, no photo, fewer than 500 connections, and free LinkedIn account. — [HELP Filters (updated 2026-08-06)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/11199236-step-2-how-to-manage-lead-list-filters)
- **Appending to an existing list** is possible only for Profile-URL lists (since August 2026). Duplicates are skipped and the list's filters are applied. — [PU Aug 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16384791-product-update-august-2026)
- **Lead Collection Report** shows found, requested, added, and skipped counts with reasons (duplicates, restrictions, unsupported profile types), plus a separate Evergreen section with a daily breakdown for post sources (since July 2026). — [HELP Report detail](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12930456-how-to-understand-the-lead-collection-report-in-detail); [PU July 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15902276-product-update-july-2026)
- **Blacklist and whitelist.** Selected leads can be blacklisted so Dripify will not process them, and they can be whitelisted again later. — [HELP Blacklist](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5029130-how-to-blacklist-certain-linkedin-users-in-dripify)
- **Duplicate message guard.** Dripify skips a lead that has already received the same message (more than 90% similar). — [HELP Duplicate messages](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5029111-how-to-avoid-sending-duplicate-messages); [HELP Failed actions](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10095836-addressing-failed-actions-on-dripify)
- **Team-wide dedupe** checks against teammates' active and inactive campaigns (since July 2022). — [HELP Team dedupe](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6367147-how-to-avoid-adding-the-same-leads-within-a-team)
- **"Add to campaign"** (June 2025, Pro and Advanced only) copies selected leads into a new or existing campaign from the Leads tab, a campaign, a list, or any sequence step. Failed leads can be re-added by failure reason. — [HELP Reuse leads](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/11680235-reuse-existing-leads-in-other-campaigns); [HELP Re-add failed](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12793373-how-to-re-add-failed-leads)
- **Lead CRM and data.** Lead data is scraped at the first action and refreshed every 30 or more days. Leads can be edited manually. The Name **Data Scrubber** (August 2023) strips terms such as "CEO" or "Dr." from names. Lead fields can be exported to CSV, including emails, phone (if it is on the profile), title, company, website, location, and last action, with no export cap. Exports over 100 leads are emailed. — [HELP Data update](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6290757-how-often-are-the-leads-data-updated-in-dripify); [HELP Scrubber](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/8193545-remove-unwanted-data-from-leads-profile-names); [HELP Export fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4914865-what-linkedin-profile-data-can-i-scrape-with-dripify); [HELP Export limit](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4914899-is-there-a-limit-on-how-many-leads-i-can-export-with-dripify)
- Marketing says "Enrich leads with ... up to 30 key attributes". — [MKT Data enrichment](https://dripify.com/features/data-enrichment/)
- **Lead tags** (Advanced only). See section 4.
- **Coming soon:** "High-intent leads caught before they go cold" is shown with a "Notify me" button on the homepage. — [MKT Homepage](https://dripify.com/)

### Inferences

- Dripify has no proprietary B2B contact database or search of its own. Every source is LinkedIn-native or brought by the user (CSV, API, MCP). One 3P review notes Dripify "does not provide data or intent signals" ([3P Gojiberry review, search snippet](https://blog.gojiberry.ai/blog/dripify-review)). This matches the homepage, where intent signals are still marked "Notify me".
- No account-level global exclusion list based on domain or company is documented. The blacklist works at the lead level.

### Gaps

- No confirmation of company-page or account (company) list imports, or of CRM imports from HubSpot into Dripify.
- The dedicated LinkedIn Events importer's limits are not documented.

## 3. Email: finding/enrichment, verification, sending in sequences, providers, credits

### Takeaway

Email is native on all paid plans. It includes a built-in Email Finder with automatic verification (100 credits per month, charged only on success), a Send Email step in sequences sent from connected **Gmail or Outlook only** (no SMTP), new-thread or same-thread follow-ups, reply detection with cross-campaign auto-pause (July 2026), bounce detection and suppression, per-step email analytics, and a DNS (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) checker. Email replies are not shown inside Dripify. Users answer from their own mailbox.

### Cited Findings

- **Email Finder.** Available as a "Find email" sequence action, or with one click from the lead profile or Leads list. Each found address is verified automatically. The average hit rate is **45–55%** ([HELP](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/7924386-how-to-search-for-a-lead-s-verified-email), updated 2025-12-05; [MKT FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/email-finder/)). This is contradicted by the same marketing page's headline claim of a "61%+ success rate" ([MKT Email finder](https://dripify.com/features/email-finder/)). A failed lookup can be retried after 30 days.
- **Credits.** Every paid plan includes 100 credits per month, which expire after 30 days and do not roll over. One credit is charged only when an email is found and verified (since June 2023). Credits are not needed to _send_ email. Enterprise gets a "Custom mail finder". — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/); [PU June 2023](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/8034245-product-update-june-2023)
- **Verification.** Automatic for Finder results only. Bulk verification of your own emails or CSV imports is **not** offered. Marketing claims "98% accuracy" and the removal of hard bounces, disposable, and catch-all addresses. — [MKT Email checker](https://dripify.com/features/email-checker/)
- **Email fields per lead:** LinkedIn email, Business (Finder) email, and Custom/manual email. Send priority is Custom, then Business, then LinkedIn. — [HELP Custom fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16051949-how-to-create-and-use-custom-lead-fields); [HELP Send email](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/9134046-how-to-send-automated-emails-via-dripify)
- **Send Email action** (Beta from May 2024). Set the subject and body, and use variables and custom fields. Body limit is 1,000 characters when using a custom variable. Each mailbox can be linked to one Dripify account. The cap is **200 emails per day on Pro and Advanced and 50 per day on Basic**, adjustable or driven by Activity Control. ## 6. Safety: cloud operation, IP/proxy, delays, daily limits, working hours, auto-pause, warnings, ban protection, extension vs cloud

## 8. Analytics & reporting

### Takeaway

Analytics are solid for a LinkedIn tool. They include an account dashboard with period comparison, daily usage, and recent activity; campaign funnels showing contacted, accepted, replied, emailed, delivered, and email-replied; a lead status bar; Step Analytics per action (Beta, Advanced); per-variant A/B stats; email step deliverability; team analytics; and CSV exports of campaign and team stats. Rates are lifetime-only, not filterable by date.

### Cited Findings

- **Account dashboard.** Invitations sent and accepted, messages, replies, endorsements, and other actions, with comparison to the previous period. Daily usage stats and a recent-activity feed that includes failures. — [HELP Account stats](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4915382-how-to-view-your-account-stats-and-actions-performed-by-dripify); [HELP Recent activity](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4915809-how-to-view-the-most-recent-actions-performed-by-dripify); [MKT Analytics](https://dripify.com/features/advanced-analytics/)
- **Campaign metrics:** total leads, contacted, accepted, replied on LinkedIn, received email, replied by email, plus a lead-loading progress bar. The **Contacted**, **Delivered**, and **Replied** email metrics were added in August 2026. — [HELP Campaign performance](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5029040-how-to-track-the-performance-of-dripify-campaigns); [PU Aug 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16384791-product-update-august-2026)
- **Lead status breakdown:** All, In progress, Completed, Paused (replied), Failed, Blacklisted, Awaiting. — [HELP Lead progress](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6481719-track-the-progress-of-your-leads-in-a-campaign)
- **Formulas.** Acceptance = accepted ÷ sent. LinkedIn response rate = responders ÷ leads reached by InMail, message, or invite. Email Replied = responders ÷ delivered. Rates **cannot be filtered by date range**, although raw counts can be filtered under Statistics. Failed actions are tracked separately. — [MKT Analytics FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/advanced-analytics/)
- **Step Analytics** (Beta from January 2026, Advanced only, campaigns created after release). Invites show sent, reply rate, and acceptance rate. Messages show sent and reply rate. Emails show sent, delivered, bounced, and reply rate. InMail shows sent and replied in the InMail article, but the analytics FAQ says InMail step analytics are "in progress", which is **inconsistent**. — [HELP Step analytics](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12742321-how-to-track-step-analytics-in-your-sequence); [PU Jan 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/13533935-product-update-january-2026); [HELP InMail](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/6067993-how-to-send-inmails-with-dripify); [MKT Analytics FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/advanced-analytics/)
- The homepage claims "Visual alerts when a step needs attention", and the analytics page says "instant alerts when results fall below average". Neither is documented in the help center. — [MKT Homepage](https://dripify.com/); [MKT Analytics](https://dripify.com/features/advanced-analytics/)
- **Exports.** Per-campaign CSV for Today, Yesterday, 30 or 90 days, or a custom range, covering invites sent and accepted, messages, InMails, replies, emails, follows, endorsements, likes, and views. Daily stats can be exported from the dashboard. A team bulk export covers selected members over a date range. — [MKT Analytics FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/advanced-analytics/); [HELP Export stats](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4915816-how-to-export-your-linkedin-statistics); [HELP Team export](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10391354-how-to-export-team-statistics-in-bulk)
- **Plan rows in the pricing table:** daily usage stats, recent activity, campaign performance reports, account performance, lead collection reports, and team analytics. Step analytics are on Advanced. — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- **Via API:** `GET /campaigns/{id}/statistics` returns the same counts and rates as the campaign screen, and team and team-member statistics are also available. — [API](https://api.dripify.com/); [HELP Open API](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16664719-open-api)

### Inferences

- There is no pipeline, revenue, or meeting attribution. Analytics stop at the reply. Tags such as "Meeting booked" exist, but no tag-based reporting is documented.

## 9. Integrations: CRM, Zapier/Make, webhooks, public API, CSV, Slack, MCP/agents

### Takeaway

The only native CRM is HubSpot, which is Beta and one-way (Dripify to HubSpot), and per the help center (updated 2026-09-25) updates existing contacts only. Other integrations are Zapier, Make, and generic webhooks, which can send multiple triggers per campaign and include the full conversation payload since 2026. An **Open API** with an API key, 60 requests per minute and 5,000 per day, launched in September 2026 on Pro and Advanced. A **remote MCP server** at `https://app.dripify.com/api/v1/mcp` works with Claude Desktop, Codex CLI, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, and Windsurf, with 4 tools. CSV import and export are available. Native Slack and Google Sheets are "Coming soon". Salesforce and Pipedrive are reached only through Zapier, Make, or webhooks.

### Cited Findings

**HubSpot (native, Beta)**

- Connect in Settings → Integrations, with one HubSpot account at a time. There are 17 mappable fields, including name, LinkedIn URL, Conversation (with the reply trigger only), country, city, position, Premium status, website, phone, company name and website, industry, employee count, followers, education, and campaign name. You can enable it per campaign alongside webhooks. Triggers are invite sent, accepted, first message sent, and request withdrawn, plus "After LinkedIn reply is received" according to the overview. You choose a HubSpot segment or static list. Contacts are matched by LinkedIn URL, then email (custom, corporate, LinkedIn), then first and last name. Email addresses are never overwritten. **"As the HubSpot integration is currently in beta, new contacts aren't created in HubSpot yet."** A disconnect banner appears, and the sync can catch up retroactively after an error. — [HELP HubSpot (updated 2026-09-25)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16198748-how-to-set-up-hubspot-integration); [HELP Integrations](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16772911-dripify-integrations)
- **Conflict:** the integrations-page FAQ says "Dripify creates the contact in HubSpot, or updates it if it already exists". The HubSpot card on the same page is still marked "Coming soon" for "Automatically create or update contacts". — [MKT Integrations](https://dripify.com/integrations/)
- HubSpot integration is included from Pro upward. — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)

**Webhooks / Zapier / Make**

- Webhooks are configured per campaign in the Settings tab and work with any public URL that accepts HTTP POST. **Multiple triggers per campaign** are allowed (since July 2026). The new trigger **"After LinkedIn reply is received"** (August 2026) sends the full LinkedIn conversation. "After lead's first LinkedIn reply" is deprecated. The reply trigger fires on the first response, and again only if the lead is resumed and replies again. Up to 16 to 22 lead data points are sent. — [HELP Step 4 webhooks](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028694-step-4-summarize-and-launch); [PU July 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/15902276-product-update-july-2026); [PU Aug 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16384791-product-update-august-2026); [HELP Webhook response](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12569521-does-every-response-trigger-the-dripify-webhook); [HELP Export fields](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4914865-what-linkedin-profile-data-can-i-scrape-with-dripify)
- The Zapier guide (updated 2026-08-05) still says "only one condition per webhook" and "lead's data ONLY ... not possible to transfer message content". This is **stale**, superseded by the July and August 2026 updates. — [HELP Zapier](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/9539864-how-to-set-up-automation-with-zapier); [HELP Make](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10260783-how-to-set-up-automation-with-make)
- Pricing lists "Webhook & Zapier integration" as a plan row. — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, and others are reached only through Zapier, Make, or webhooks. — [MKT Integrations FAQ](https://dripify.com/integrations/); [MKT Personalization FAQ](https://dripify.com/features/hyper-personalized-outreach/)

**Open API (September 2026; Pro and Advanced)**

- Help center scope: list campaigns and statistics, campaign lead lists, leads by status, lead detail, add leads to a campaign, and teams and members with stats. Generate or revoke the key in Settings → Integrations. Listed as "coming" in the help article: activity log, messaging history, sending messages, tags, and custom fields. — [HELP Open API (updated 2026-09-11)](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16664719-open-api); [PU Sep 2026](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16938124-product-update-september-2026)
- **The live API reference already includes more** than the help article, under `/v1/open-api/`:
  - Leads: list (filter by campaign, list, status, or tag; cursor pagination up to 100), search by email or LinkedIn URL, get by ID, **activity log**, **conversation**, and **send a message into an existing LinkedIn conversation** (up to 5,000 characters; it cannot start new conversations and it pauses the lead's campaigns).
  - Custom lead field definitions, plus set or unset a value on a lead.
  - Conversation messages.
  - Bulk apply or remove tags.
  - Campaigns: list, lead lists, statistics, upload leads (1 to 1,000 per request, LinkedIn URL or publicId, always creating a new list), and append leads to an existing list.
  - Tags: list and create.
  - Teams and team members.
  - Rate limits are **60 requests per minute and 5,000 per day per key**. Auth uses the `X-Api-Key` header. Activating or deactivating campaigns is **not** part of v1. The doc header oddly still calls it a "Read-only public API".
    — [API reference](https://api.dripify.com/)

**MCP server (help article dated 2026-09-28; Pro and Advanced)**

- The endpoint `https://app.dripify.com/api/v1/mcp` uses streamable HTTP and authenticates with an `X-Api-Key` header (OAuth is planned). It has 4 tools: list campaigns (status DRAFT, NOT_ACTIVE, or ACTIVE, plus a locked flag), list a campaign's lead lists, search leads by email or LinkedIn URL (errors above 100 matches), and upload up to 1,000 LinkedIn profile URLs as a new list (no Sales Navigator or Recruiter URLs). The upload is write-capable, and the recommended setup is read tools "Always allow" and write tools "Needs approval". Supported clients are Claude Desktop (custom connector with request headers, or the mcp-remote bridge), Codex CLI, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, and Windsurf. Claude web or mobile and ChatGPT are **not yet supported** because they require a different auth method. The lead-upload tool is "still rolling out". — [HELP MCP](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/17171745-how-to-connect-dripify-to-ai-assistants-with-the-mcp-server); [HELP Integrations](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/16772911-dripify-integrations); [MKT Integrations FAQ](https://dripify.com/integrations/)
- Pricing lists "Open API & MCP Server" under the Pro plan. — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- **Stale vendor claim:** the Waalaxy comparison table still says "MCP: In progress". — [MKT Waalaxy comparison](https://dripify.com/comparison/waalaxy-vs-dripify/)

**Other**

- **Slack** ("real-time Slack alerts") and **Google Spreadsheet** (push lead data) are both marked **"Coming soon / Notify me"**. **Google** sign-up and Gmail are live. — [MKT Integrations](https://dripify.com/integrations/)
- CSV import and export are available on all plans, per the pricing table rows "Import leads" and "Export leads into CSV". — [Pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)

### Inferences

- Agent or automation builders can read campaign and lead state and push leads and LinkedIn messages through the API. However, campaign creation, sequence editing, and campaign start or stop are UI-only. There is no outbound event stream except per-campaign webhooks.

### Gaps

- Webhook payload signing or verification is not documented. The full list of webhook triggers is not enumerated in one place. The ones mentioned are invite sent, invite accepted, message sent, profile viewed, reply received, and request withdrawn.

### Inferences

- Dripify's 2026 direction is clear: catch up on multichannel email (reply and bounce detection, analytics), team structure, and developer or agent access (API and MCP). The AI work is light and limited to copy-editing. Signals and intent sourcing and AI personalization are announced but not shipped.
- There is a lot of doc drift between marketing pages, older help articles, and the changelog, covering variable counts, InMail limits, HubSpot contact creation, email-in-inbox, Zapier trigger limits, and MCP status. Parity claims should be pinned to the [HELP], [PU], and [API] sources and their dates.

### Gaps

- There is no public, dated changelog outside the help-center "Product Update" posts. The October 2022 Announcements sidebar exists inside the app but was not accessible.
- Whether "Improve with AI" is available on every plan is not stated.
- YouTube tutorials were not reviewed. The help center links many tutorial videos (for example the sequence builder at https://youtu.be/dVOA6iU9K5s), but their dates are unknown.
