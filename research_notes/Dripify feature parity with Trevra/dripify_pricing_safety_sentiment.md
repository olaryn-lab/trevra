# Dripify (dripify.com, formerly dripify.io): pricing, company, safety and compliance, and user sentiment as of 20 October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-20. "Live" means fetched directly on 2026-10-20. Dates for older data points are given inline. dripify.io now redirects to dripify.com. The first Wayback capture of dripify.com/pricing is 2025-07-09, so the domain move happened around July 2025 ([Wayback CDX for dripify.com/pricing](https://web.archive.org/web/20250709021759/https://dripify.com/pricing/)).

Method notes: I read Dripify's own pages directly (pricing, ToS, two privacy policies, Trust Center, help center, careers site, Chrome Web Store listing). I used Wayback Machine snapshots to build the price history. G2 and Trustpilot block automated fetches (G2 returned HTTP 403; Trustpilot returned 403 and is not archived), so figures for those two come from search-result snippets and third-party write-ups and are marked as such. Reddit is not reachable with my tools, so Reddit sentiment is second-hand only.

## Pricing: plans, prices, seat model, trial, add-ons, and 2022-2026 history

### Takeaway

As of 20 October 2026 Dripify sells two self-serve per-seat plans plus Enterprise: Pro at $79/month ($59/month billed annually) and Advanced at $99/month ($79/month billed annually). One seat connects one LinkedIn account. Headline prices have not changed since at least July 2022. The entry-level Basic plan ($59, or $39 annual) was still listed on 3 October 2026 but was gone by 20 October 2026, so the entry price rose about 34% monthly and 51% annual in October 2026. Features have also moved up-tier over time: A/B testing sat in Pro in 2022-2024 and is now Advanced-only.

### Cited Findings

**Current plans (live, 2026-10-20)**

- The pricing page lists three tiers: Pro, Advanced ("most popular"), and Enterprise. There is no Basic tier and no free plan. Toggle text reads "Monthly / Annually up to 35% off". — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Pro costs $79 per user per month, or $59 billed annually. It is positioned "For sellers launching LinkedIn and email campaigns that get replies". It includes LinkedIn and email sequences, 100 email-finder credits, unlimited drip campaigns, "Built-in LinkedIn protection", analytics and reports, a dedicated inbox, HubSpot integration, "Open API & MCP Server", and 24/7 live support. — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Advanced costs $99 per user per month, or $79 billed annually. It covers everything in Pro, plus team management, "Advanced LinkedIn protection", lead tagging, step analytics, A/B testing, and "Evergreen leads". The FAQ calls it "our most popular plan" and says "It's also the plan you get in the free trial". — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Enterprise is custom-priced ("Contact sales") "based on seat count". It adds premium onboarding, dedicated technical support, a customer success manager, and a "Custom mail finder". Teams with 10 or more seats, or those consolidating several subscriptions into one invoice, are told to contact sales. — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Seat model, quoted from the FAQ: "Each Dripify seat connects one LinkedIn account. A team of five reps needs five seats." Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply from the next billing cycle. — [Dripify pricing FAQ (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Daily quotas listed for both Pro and Advanced: 75 connection requests, "100+" messages, 30 Sales Navigator InMails, "200+" profile views, and "50+" each of endorsements, likes, and follows. — [Dripify pricing comparison table (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Email-finder credits:
  - Every plan includes 100 credits per month. They are "valid for 30 days and not rolled over" and charged only when an address is found and verified.
  - Extra packs cost $29 for 1,000, $49 for 2,000, $69 for 5,000, and $99 for 10,000.
  - Quoted from the FAQ: "Sending isn't metered on any plan."
    — [Dripify pricing FAQ (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- LinkedIn Premium or Sales Navigator is not required ("works with all LinkedIn account types including free LinkedIn accounts"). Sales Navigator is described as a separate LinkedIn subscription. — [Dripify pricing FAQ (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Trial: 7 days, no credit card, with Advanced-plan features. Quoted from the FAQ: "When the trial ends, nothing is charged automatically." — [Dripify pricing FAQ (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/); the ToS also says "We offer a 7-day free trial" — [Dripify Terms](https://dripify.com/terms-and-conditions/)
- Cancellation and refunds:
  - Plans auto-renew.
  - From the ToS: "All monthly purchases are non-refundable. If you subscribe to one of our annual plans, you are eligible for a refund within 24 hours after we receive your payment."
  - A cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid term.
  - The "LinkedIn profile makeover service" is non-refundable.
    — [Dripify Terms](https://dripify.com/terms-and-conditions/)
- Other paid services: a "LinkedIn profile makeover" service, and a "custom masterclass" request form whose confirmation message says the sales team will "personalize your quote". — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/); [Dripify sitemap: /linkedin-profile-makeover/](https://dripify.com/linkedin-profile-makeover/)
- Odd detail: the live pricing page still contains an "A/B test your message: Sign up here to be first in line when our exciting new feature rolls out" waitlist modal, while also listing A/B testing as an Advanced feature. — [Dripify pricing (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)

**Affiliate and channel economics**

- The affiliate program pays recurring commission described as "20-35% recurring — no reset after 12 months, no cliffs": 35% of the first monthly purchase and 20% of renewals. The annual-plan rate is also listed. An agency program is linked from the navigation. — [Dripify Affiliate Program](https://dripify.com/affiliate-program/)

### Inferences

- The "up to 35% off" annual claim only held for the old Basic plan ($59 to $39 is about 34%). For the remaining plans the real annual discount is about 25% for Pro ($79 to $59) and about 20% for Advanced ($99 to $79). That makes the headline claim misleading now that Basic is gone. (My calculation from the listed prices.)
- Removing Basic raised the cheapest way in from $39 to $59 per month on annual billing (+51%) and from $59 to $79 on monthly billing (+34%), with headline prices unchanged since 2022. This looks like monetization through packaging, not list-price changes. A/B testing moving from Pro (2022-2024) to Advanced (2026) fits the same pattern.
- Per-LinkedIn-account pricing with no pooled or shared-seat option makes multi-account agencies expensive. Five seats on Advanced cost $395 per month annual or $495 monthly, before Sales Navigator. That is a gap a workspace-priced competitor could exploit.
- A 24-hour refund window on annual plans, plus non-refundable monthly plans and auto-renewal, is buyer-hostile. It underlies the billing complaints in the sentiment section.
- "Open API & MCP Server" appearing in Pro in October 2026 shows Dripify is positioning for agent-driven use. Because sending runs through the LinkedIn account Dripify controls, agent-triggered sends would carry the same risk under LinkedIn's terms.

### Gaps

- I couldn't confirm the exact date Basic was removed (sometime between the 3 October and 20 October 2026 captures), or whether existing Basic subscribers were grandfathered.
- The annual-plan affiliate commission rate was truncated in my extraction.
- Enterprise price points are not published, and I found no reliable third-party quotes.
- I found no evidence of a free plan after 2021. A 2025 Capterra reviewer mentions "the free version", which most likely means the trial.

## Company: owner/operator, HQ, founding, team size, funding, user claims, target customers

### Takeaway

Dripify is a privately held, remote-first company with a mostly Ukrainian team. Third-party databases place it in Kyiv, founded in 2019. Its careers site says "Founded in 2017" with "100+" coworkers. Its public legal documents do not name a legal entity, registered address, or governing law. I found no disclosed founder or CEO and no disclosed funding. User claims range from "thousands of companies in 72+ countries" on the pricing page to "40,000+ companies" on the careers site.

## Safety and compliance: how it operates, credentials, LinkedIn's rules and enforcement, GDPR, certifications, data location

### Takeaway

Dripify is a cloud tool. Campaigns run on Dripify's AWS-hosted servers, each account gets a "dedicated" local-region IP, and actions use randomized, working-hours pacing. Users connect LinkedIn either by giving Dripify their LinkedIn email and password or through a new Chrome extension that captures the logged-in session; Dripify also collects "social networking cookies". This is exactly what LinkedIn's User Agreement §8.2 prohibits.

Dripify's help center nevertheless tells users the product will "ensure you won't get flagged" and is "completely safe to use". Its own training content covers "creating a bot account on LinkedIn" and "safe ways to bypass" the weekly invite limit. Its LinkedIn company page was named with Cyrillic homoglyphs ("Drіріfу") and now returns 404.

Compliance posture is self-attested: GDPR-aligned, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2/1.3, SCCs, with the DPA and subprocessor list available only on request. No SOC 2 or ISO 27001 is claimed.

### Cited Findings

**How Dripify operates (Dripify's own claims)**

  - "Cloud sending – campaigns run on our servers".
  - "Human-like behaviour – actions are spaced with randomised timing and run only inside the working hours you set".
  - "Adaptive activity control – your sending pace adjusts to your account's own activity".
    — [Dripify pricing FAQ (live)](https://dripify.com/pricing/)
- Help center: "Dripify makes this possible by assigning a unique IP address from your local region to your Dripify account ... Dripify is designed to perform automated LinkedIn actions in the cloud, making the software completely safe to use." The same article promises safety measures "to ensure you won't get flagged by LinkedIn" and says "your LinkedIn account is completely secure" (article dated 7 July 2025). — [Help: How can I be sure my account won't get blocked](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5030328-how-can-i-be-sure-my-account-won-t-get-blocked-by-linkedin)

- A competitor's June 2026 test reportedly found Dripify's "shared-provider datacenter IPs rated 94/100 for fraud risk". That contradicts the "unique local IP" marketing, but it comes from a competitor and the page would not load for me. — [Linked Helper review of Dripify (competitor; search snippet only)](https://www.linkedhelper.com/reviews/dripify-review)

**GDPR, data processing, certifications, data location**

- Trust Center badges: "GDPR-aligned processing", "AES-256 encryption at rest", "TLS 1.2/1.3 in transit", "Least-privilege access", "SCC-based transfers", "DPA available on request", "No data sold, ever", "Corporate insurance coverage". — [Dripify Trust Center](https://dripify.com/trust-center/)
- Processor and controller roles: for lead data Dripify acts as processor, and "The customer remains responsible for determining the purposes and lawful basis of the relevant processing activities, including the collection, use, and communication of lead data." From the same page: "we do not use it to train AI models"; lead data is not pooled across customers. — [Dripify Trust Center](https://dripify.com/trust-center/)
- The DPA is provided "to customers who require one" on request. The subprocessor list is "available upon request" and not public. Subprocessor categories listed: cloud hosting, identity and authentication, payments, CRM and marketing automation, transactional email, and email verification. — [Dripify Trust Center](https://dripify.com/trust-center/); [Dripify Customer Privacy Policy §15](https://dripify.com/privacy-policy/customer/)
- Data location: "countries outside the European Union and the European Economic Area, such as the United States, where Our infrastructure and certain Sub-processors are located", with transfers under adequacy or SCCs. — [Dripify Customer Privacy Policy](https://dripify.com/privacy-policy/customer/)
- Retention:
  - Inactive accounts: data deleted after up to 180 days.
  - Active accounts: no longer than 3 years after last use.
  - Deletion within 1 month of a valid request.
  - Billing data kept up to 7 years.
    — [Dripify Customer Privacy Policy](https://dripify.com/privacy-policy/customer/); the Trust Center says up to three years after last use — [Dripify Trust Center](https://dripify.com/trust-center/)
- Certifications: neither the Trust Center nor the customer privacy policy mentions SOC 2, ISO 27001, or penetration testing. A text search of both pages found no such terms. Security measures are self-described: RBAC, logging, monitoring, endpoint protection, staff GDPR training, and "Internal security review". — [Dripify Trust Center](https://dripify.com/trust-center/)
- The older website privacy policy names no entity or data location and says data is "stored and managed on our service providers' servers". — [Dripify Privacy Policy](https://dripify.com/privacy-policy/)
- Google API compliance: Dripify says it complies with Google's Limited Use policy for Gmail access used for "reading and sending emails". — [Dripify Terms](https://dripify.com/terms-and-conditions/)

### Inferences

- Dripify's core value (cloud execution under the user's own LinkedIn identity, from a residential-looking IP) is the behavior LinkedIn §8.2 forbids. Its marketing ("ensure you won't get flagged", "completely safe") overpromises against LinkedIn's explicit statement that such tools risk restriction. A compliance-first competitor can frame this as an honesty gap.
- The homoglyph company-page name and the "bot account" and "bypass the weekly invite limit" training topics show Dripify deliberately evading LinkedIn's detection. The company page now returns 404 after LinkedIn removed or restricted Apollo, Seamless (March 2025) and HeyReach (March 2026). It is plausible that LinkedIn removed Dripify's page too, but no source confirms the cause (Dripify could have deleted or renamed it).
- The new Chrome-extension connection method (2026) shifts risk from password sharing to session capture. It also makes Dripify users detectable by LinkedIn's extension probing if the extension ID is on LinkedIn's list. I could not confirm whether it is.
- Dripify says the LinkedIn password is "encrypted" (help center) in one place and "used for one-time authentication only and is not stored" (privacy policy) in another. Either way it keeps session cookies, so it effectively holds bearer access to the user's LinkedIn account. A credential-free or human-in-the-loop design avoids that liability.
- On GDPR, Dripify puts the lawful-basis burden for scraped LinkedIn lead data on the customer. With no SOC 2, no public subprocessor list, and no named legal entity, mid-market and EU procurement teams have little to rely on. A competitor with a named DPA, public subprocessors, and audit attestations wins on compliance review.

### Gaps

- I couldn't confirm the reason Dripify's LinkedIn company page now returns 404, or whether LinkedIn has taken any action naming Dripify specifically. No lawsuit or public LinkedIn statement about Dripify was found.
- The 40% Q1-2026 restriction rate and the 94/100 IP fraud-score test both come from competitors and are unverified.
- AWS region(s) and the actual subprocessor names are not disclosed publicly; the list is available on request only.
- Whether Dripify's extension ID appears in LinkedIn's BrowserGate probe list remains unverified (I did not locate the list).
- I found no official LinkedIn documentation of the weekly invitation cap or any 2024-2026 change to it.

## Churn reasons and where users go

### Takeaway

The documented churn drivers are price relative to realized output, tier gating, auto-renewal and refund friction, LinkedIn restrictions, and volume falling short of marketing claims. Inbound switching is well documented: Capterra reviewers came to Dripify from PhantomBuster, Octopus CRM, Expandi, and Waalaxy. Outbound switching destinations are poorly documented. Dripify's own comparison pages show which rivals it treats as its set: Dux-Soup, Expandi, Meet Alfred, PhantomBuster, Linked Helper, Waalaxy, and HeyReach.
